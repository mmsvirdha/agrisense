How this reading works

Detection uses HSV color segmentation and contour shape checks — not a trained neural network. It works best on clear, close-up shots of round fruit against a plain background. Maturity is a peel-color estimate; the health flag only signals dark or uneven patches. Neither is a species ID or disease diagnosis.
